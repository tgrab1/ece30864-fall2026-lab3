QUESTION C:

The three types of meetings during a spring cycle are a Sprint Planning Meeting, a Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting, and a Sprint Retrospective meeting.

Sprint Planning Meeting:
- Determine what to complete in the coming Sprint
- Move stories in sprint scope from backlog to current sprint

Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
- Team members discuss the work completed and work plan for the day 
- Team members also discuss any blockers which are impacting progress.

Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
- Discuss work completed and move incomplete work to the backlog
- Identify areas of improvement and create action items
